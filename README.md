## 📖 Abstract

This master's thesis investigates the integration of **Radar sensors** with **Artificial Intelligence** to develop robust **Detect and Avoid (DAA)** systems.

## 🗂️ Thesis Structure & Artifacts

The project is organized into modular phases, reflecting the academic milestones of the Master's program.

### 📍 [1. Qualification Phase (Systematic Review)](./qualification/README.md)

**Status: ✅ Completed**

Comprehensive systematic literature review following PRISMA guidelines to map the state-of-the-art in Radar-based DAA.

- **Scoping Review:** Preliminary exploration to identify taxonomy and define the search strategy.
- **Systematic Literature Review:** A rigorous PRISMA-based mapping of the state-of-the-art (529 papers analyzed → 9 included studies).
- **Research Design:** Definition of the thesis methodology, gap analysis, and the development project plan.

### 📍 2. Development (Upcoming)

**Status: 🚧 In Progress**

Implementation of the proposed solution.

### 📍 3. Dissemination & Defense (Upcoming)

**Status: 📅 Planned**

Consolidation of results for academic contribution and final degree requirements.

- **Scientific Paper:** Drafting and submission of articles to conferences (e.g., IEEE RadarConf, AIAA) or journals.
- **Thesis Defense:** Public presentation and defense of the research findings to the examination board.
- **Final Deliverables:** Publication of the approved thesis text and open-sourcing of the dataset/codebase.
